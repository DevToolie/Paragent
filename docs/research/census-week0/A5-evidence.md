# A5 — Frequency & Pain Evidence

**Role:** Independent evidence-gatherer for frequency and pain (not browser-only, not scoring).  
**Access date for all citations:** 2026-07-24  
**Inputs:** `census/A1-datadog.md`, `census/A2-grafana.md`, `census/A3-sentry.md`  
**Method:** Community/support forums, Q&A, vendor docs/changelogs, eng blogs, help-center articles. Recurring “how do I / this broke / automate this” threads treated as strongest available proxy for frequency × pain.  
**Hard rule:** Absence of evidence ≠ absence of the task. Where searches returned nothing usable, row says `no evidence found` with queries tried.

### Frequency column convention
- Numeric estimates are **per customer org per month**, with explicit basis.  
- `no evidence found` = no credible external proxy for how often a given customer performs this task.  
- One-time / rare onboarding tasks may show `~0–0.5` with basis “once per org or per new env/account,” not monthly habit.

### Confidence
- **HIGH** = multiple independent community/docs signals aligned.  
- **MED** = at least one solid primary source + reasonable inference.  
- **LOW** = thin signal; estimate or paraphrase should be treated as provisional.
